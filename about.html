Our Heritage Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Dr. Sudarshan Murthy's unwavering ethics, deep spiritual values, devotion to patient care and profound knowledge of Ayurveda have earned him recognition among India's most respected Ayurvedic physicians. Inspired and encouraged by renowned senior Ayurvedic scholars and legends, he has devoted his life to preserving and promoting the rich heritage of Ayurveda.

Over the years, he has delivered numerous lectures and keynote addresses at National and International conferences, sharing the scientific relevance and practical applications of Ayurveda with medical professionals and researchers worldwide.

National & International Recognition

Dr. Sudarshan Murthy has been honoured for his outstanding contributions to Ayurveda and recognized by the All India Ayurved Congress - New Delhi for his distinguished service to the profession.

He has been invited to participate in several prestigious national conferences inaugurated by eminent dignitaries, including:

  • Hon'ble Prime Minister Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee
  • Former Prime Minister Shri P. V. Narasimha Rao
  • Former Vice President Shri Krishan Kant
  • Hon'ble President of India Smt. Pratibha Patil
  • Hon'ble President of India Shri Pranab Mukherjee

He has also represented Ayurveda at numerous international conferences that encourage scientific exchange among physicians, researchers, scholars and healthcare professionals from across the world.
