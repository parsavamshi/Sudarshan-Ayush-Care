Our Heritage

The legacy of Ayurveda in our family began in 1863, when Sri Ramajogi Zamindar established his Ayurvedic practice in Tatiparthy Village, near Pithapuram, East Godavari District, Andhra Pradesh. Renowned for his exceptional knowledge and compassionate healing, he was honored with the prestigious title "Vaidya Siromani."

For over five generations, our family has proudly continued this noble tradition of authentic Ayurvedic healing, preserving the wisdom of classical Ayurveda while serving society with dedication and compassion.

Our Journey

Sudarshan AYUSH CARE was established in 1978 at Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, by Dr. Sudarshan Murthy, one of South India's respected Ayurvedic physicians.

Dr. Sudarshan Murthy began his training in Ayurveda from his childhood under the guidance of his father "Vaidya Raja" Sree Ramulu garu the founder of Sree Rama Jogi Ayurveda Nilayam (Regd.) a renowned manufacturing of 260 sastric Ayurveda and proprietary (patient) medicines.

Continuing this rich heritage, Dr. Sudarshan Murthy also established Sri Raghavendra Pharmaceuticals, manufacturing authentic Ayurvedic classical medicines. Later, he dedicated himself entirely to clinical practice, providing personalized Ayurvedic healthcare and helping thousands of patients achieve better health through natural healing.

With decades of clinical experience and extensive knowledge of Ayurvedic herbs, medicines, and therapies, he continues to offer holistic treatments focused on restoring health and improving quality of life.

Our Vision

At Sudarshan AYUSH CARE, our mission is to help individuals unlock their true health potential through the timeless wisdom of Ayurveda.

We are committed to:

  • Promoting holistic health through authentic Ayurvedic treatments
  • Providing personalized healthcare based on classical Ayurvedic principles
  • Encouraging healthy living through proper diet, lifestyle, and natural healing
  • Practicing Ayurveda according to the timeless wisdom of the Vedas
  • Delivering compassionate, ethical, and evidence-informed Ayurvedic care

Our goal is to empower every individual to live a healthier, happier, and more meaningful life through natural wellness.

Recognition & Achievements

Dr. Sudarshan Murthy has actively participated in numerous National and International Health Conferences and Medical Organizations, contributing to the advancement and promotion of Ayurveda.

In recognition of his outstanding service to humanity through Ayurveda, he was honored with the prestigious "Swarna Simha Talatam Puraskar," a centuries-old royal traditional honor presented for exceptional dedication to Ayurvedic healthcare.
